About

I am a final-year Finance undergraduate at Shihezi University (211), with a GPA of 3.91/5 (89.12/100) and a professional ranking of 15/74. From September 2024 to July 2025, I completed a one-year exchange study at South China Agricultural University with a GPA of 3.93/5.

I have built strong quantitative foundations through coursework in Advanced Mathematics, Statistics, Econometrics, Python, and Big Data Financial Analysis, and I am proficient in Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib), Stata, SQL, SPSS, and Excel. I also have hands-on experience with machine learning methods such as logistic regression, decision trees, PCA, K-means, and regularization techniques including VIF diagnostics, winsorization, and cross-validation.

In my research and internships, I developed risk-control models and analyzed financial data end to end: I built a quarterly financial panel of 80 A-share new energy listed companies and developed a credit risk-screening model that achieved a test-set AUC of 0.762, and I surveyed frontline staff and mid-level managers at small and medium-sized commercial banks across Fujian for an industry research report. Through internships at Fujian Youth Development Foundation and Fuzhou Rural Commercial Bank, I standardized multi-year scholarship data for the foundation's longitudinal dataset and audited 150+ business vouchers against compliance checklists. I also earned a National Second Prize (top 1% of teams nationwide) in the Eastmoney 3rd National ETF Elite Challenge by constructing an ETF portfolio that returned 9% over approximately two months.
