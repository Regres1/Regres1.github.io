About

I am a final-year Finance undergraduate at Shihezi University (GPA 89.12/100, 3.91/5), with a one-year exchange study at South China Agricultural University from September 2024 to July 2025.

These experiences motivate me to deepen data science training at CityU (Dongguan) toward a fintech career.
